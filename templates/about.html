Berkeley Choice

An AI-powered course recommendation system tailored to each UC Berkeley student’s academic profile and career goals.

🎯 Why We Built This

Choosing the right courses is challenging. With thousands of options and ever-evolving career demands, students often lack guidance on which classes will truly support their future. Berkeley Choice bridges this gap through intelligent, skill-based recommendations personalized for every student.

🚀 Our AI-Driven Recommendation Pipeline

Here’s how we turn student data into precise, personalized course recommendations:

  • Skill Clustering: We analyzed 10,000+ raw skills from industry/job data and grouped them into ~800 skill clusters using advanced clustering algorithms. These clusters form a universal skill vocabulary for mapping job roles.
  • Neural Skill Prediction: A neural network trained on 10,000+ successful UC Berkeley student profiles predicts the most relevant skill clusters based on a student's academic background, past experience, and career aspirations — achieving over 90% accuracy and high F1-scores.
  • Semantic Matching via Sentence-BERT: Skill clusters and course descriptions are embedded using a fine-tuned Sentence-BERT model under a semi-supervised learning framework, enabling deep understanding of course relevance.
  • Personalized Course Matching: Each student’s skill profile is compared against 10,000+ UC Berkeley courses to identify the top matches, providing transparent, data-driven suggestions tailored just for them.
🎯 What Makes Us Different?
  • 🔬 Personalized Recommendations: No two students are treated the same. Your unique goals and background shape your results.
  • 📊 Data-Driven Insights: We use real alumni success profiles to guide and validate every recommendation.
  • 💬 Explainable Matches: Each course includes the reason it's recommended — so you know how it supports your skills and goals.
🔧 What’s Next?

We’re expanding to more universities and refining our models to include real-time job market insights and employer demand. Our mission is to empower every student with data-backed academic decisions.
