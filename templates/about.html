Berkeley Choice

An AI-powered course recommendation system tailored to each UC Berkeley student’s academic profile and career goals.

Why We Built This

Choosing the right courses is challenging. With thousands of options and ever-evolving career demands, students often lack guidance on which classes will truly support their future. Berkeley Choice bridges this gap through intelligent, skill-based recommendations personalized for every student.

Our AI-Driven Recommendation Pipeline

A step-by-step transformation of student data into personalized course recommendations.

Profile Collection

We curated over 10,000+ real student profiles from open datasets, capturing academic records, personal experiences, and career ambitions to form the foundation of our system.

Skill Clustering

Using HDBSCAN, we clustered thousands of raw skills into 500+ target skillsets, ensuring a clean and structured vocabulary across student and job data.

Neural Skill Prediction

A multi-layer perceptron (MLP) model analyzes academic and personal data to predict relevant skill clusters, identifying potential gaps and opportunities tailored to each student.

Course Dataset Aggregation

We scraped and processed 10,000+ UC Berkeley course descriptions, combining them with job listings and student resumes for deep semantic enrichment.

Semantic Skill-Course Labeling

A custom Word2Vec model was trained on a combination of resumes, job posts, and course descriptions. It generated match scores to label skill-course pairs.

Sentence-BERT Fine-Tuning

We fine-tuned Sentence-BERT on 20,000+ labeled skill–course examples using cosine similarity loss, resulting in highly contextual embeddings for recommendation.

Personalized Recommendation Engine

Given a student profile, our NLP engine matches predicted skills to course descriptions. If a resume is uploaded, we extract known skills and compute a skill-fit score showing alignment with each course.

What Makes Us Different?

Berkeley Choice is not just another recommendation engine — it’s a transparent, skill-aligned platform designed for students' success.

  • Personalized: Every recommendation is tailored to your goals and background.
  • Data-Driven: Grounded in real student success stories and outcome data.
  • Explainable: Understand why each course is recommended — no black boxes.
What’s Next?

We’re expanding to more universities and refining our models to include real-time job market insights and employer demand. Our mission is to empower every student with data-backed academic decisions.
